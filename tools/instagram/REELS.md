# Anqah Tech: Reel scripts

> Arabic version for the filming team: REELS-AR.md (النسخة العربية)

There are 14 Reels, one for each feed post topic. Each Reel goes up **the day after** its matching post, on the days between feed posts, so you post something every day. ⭐ marks the 6 most important ones; if 14 is too many, film those first.

## Before you film (applies to every Reel)
- **Format:** vertical **9:16 (1080×1920)**, 7–25 seconds. Shorter Reels get watched to the end more often, which earns more reach.
- **Hook in the first second:** big on-screen text plus movement. Never open on the logo.
- **Text position:** keep text in the middle of the frame. The top 220px and bottom 420px are covered by Instagram's buttons and caption.
- **Light:** film near a window or under bright shop light. Clean the phone lens first.
- **Editing:** use **Instagram's own editor or CapCut**. Add on-screen text in **English and Arabic**, one line each.
- **Audio:** pick a **trending sound** in the Reels audio browser (look for the ↗ arrow). Keep music low if there is voice-over.
- **Cover:** use the ready-made covers `out/reel-01-….png` to `out/reel-14-….png` (1080×1920). When posting, tap **Edit cover → Add from camera roll**. The profile grid shows only the centre 3:4 of the cover, so the titles sit in the middle.
- **Caption:** 1–2 lines, then a CTA keyword ("Comment POS"), then 3–5 hashtags and a location.
- **Bilingual caption:** paste **Caption (EN)**, then a `—` line, then **Caption (AR)**. Instagram allows 5 hashtags in total, so keep the 2 Arabic ones and 3 English ones.
- **Feed:** turn on *Also share to feed*. Use **Trial Reels** (sent to non-followers only) for the ones you're unsure about.
- **Faces:** only film customers' shops, staff or faces **with their permission**. Blur screens that show real sales or personal data.

## After posting
- **Within 48 hours:** check **Insights → Reels**. Look at **watch time** and **skip rate**. If people leave before 3 seconds, the hook needs to be stronger.
- **Repost winners:** take your top 2 Reels and repost them a month later with a new hook and a different audio track.
