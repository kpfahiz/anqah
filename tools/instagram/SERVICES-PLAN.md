# Anqah Tech: service posts plan (24 Oct to 7 Nov 2026)

This continues after the launch timeline (TIMELINE.md ends on Thu 22 Oct).
- **Images:** `out/s1-…png` to `out/s8-…png`. Each post has an English and an Arabic (`-ar`) version.
- **Source:** `services.html`. Re-render with `node tools/instagram/render_posts.mjs <node_modules path>`.

## How to post every one of these
- **Format:** a 2-slide carousel: English first, then Arabic. Choose the **4:5** crop.
- **Time:** **8:00 pm UAE / 7:00 pm KSA**. On Fridays, post at **8:30 pm**.
- **Caption:** English caption, then a `—` line, then the Arabic caption. Use at most 5 hashtags.
- **Order:** the posts alternate light and dark themes, so the grid never shows two similar posts next to each other.

## Extra reach checklist (do this on every post)
1. **Location:** set it as in the table below.
2. **Music:** tap *Add music* and choose a trending, low-volume track. Carousels with music can also appear in the Reels feed.
3. **Alt text:** *Advanced settings → Accessibility → Alt text*. Paste the alt text below. It helps Instagram understand the post and show it in search.
4. **Pinned first comment:** post the "first comment" line below and pin it. It adds search keywords and gives people something to reply to.
5. **Facebook:** turn on *Share to Facebook* if you have a Facebook page. You get two audiences from one post.
6. **First hour:** reply to every comment within 1 hour. Send each "WEB / CCTV / APP …" commenter a DM.
7. **Story:** share the post to your Story the same night with a poll sticker. The poll question is listed for each post below.
8. **Collab:** if a client is featured or helped, invite them as a **Collaborator**. The post then shows on both accounts.
