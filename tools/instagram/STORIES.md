# Anqah Tech: Story templates

> Arabic version: STORIES-AR.md (النسخة العربية)

All images are 1080×1920 and in `out/`. Source: `stories.html`. Re-render with `render_posts.mjs`.
Instagram's buttons cover the top ~250px and bottom ~340px, so every template keeps its content between them.

## 1 · Post share frames (`story-share-*.png`, 17 images)
One for each feed post, in that post's colours, with the poll question already printed on it.

**How to use one (on post day, right after posting):**
1. Open your new post and tap **✈️ Share → Add to story**.
2. Tap the **background picker** (top), choose **Camera roll**, and pick the matching `story-share-…png`.
   *Or start a Story from the frame image, then add the post with the **Sticker → Feed post** option, if your app shows it.*
3. Pinch the post sticker to fit the empty rounded box.
4. Add a **Poll sticker** under the printed question:
   - Options in English, e.g. "Yes 👍 / Not yet". Arabic viewers still understand the printed Arabic question.
5. Add a **Location sticker** (same city as the post) and a **Link sticker** to anqah.com if you like.

| Post | Frame |
|---|---|
| 01 Hello … 09 Contact | `story-share-01-hello` … `story-share-09-contact` |
| Web design … Engineering | `story-share-s1-web-design` … `story-share-s8-engineering` |

## 2 · Reusable templates (`story-tpl-*.png`, 7 images)

| Template | Use it for | What to add in Instagram |
|---|---|---|
| `story-tpl-poll` | Quick "A or B" questions ("Card or cash?", "Website or app first?") | Text sticker with the question + **Poll** sticker |
| `story-tpl-ask-us` | Weekly Q&A, every Thursday | **Questions** sticker over the empty box. Answer each question as a new Story |
| `story-tpl-tech-tip` | A weekly tip ("Back up your POS data daily") | Type the tip over the code box |
| `story-tpl-install-done` | Every finished job | A photo of the install over the black box + a **Location** sticker. Tag the client if they agree |
| `story-tpl-client-review` | Screenshots or quotes from happy clients | The review text + the client's name (with permission) |
| `story-tpl-before-after` | CCTV, network or website upgrades | 2 photos: old setup on top, new one below |
| `story-tpl-free-demo` | Ready to post as is, about once a week | A **Link** sticker to WhatsApp or anqah.com |

## 3 · Highlight covers (`story-hl-*.png`, 7 images)
**SELLO 🧾 · BELL 🔔 · WATER 💧 · SERVICES 🛠️ · INSTALLS 📸 · CLIENTS ⭐ · CONTACT 📲**

**Create each Highlight:**
1. Profile → **+ New** → pick the Stories to include.
2. Name it, e.g. "SELLO" or "التركيب".
3. **Edit cover** → choose the matching `story-hl-…png`. Instagram shows the centre circle.

**Suggested order on the profile:** CONTACT · SELLO · SERVICES · INSTALLS · CLIENTS · BELL · WATER

## Weekly Story rhythm (1–3 Stories a day)

| Day | Story |
|---|---|
| Post days | Share frame + poll (see TIMELINE.md / SERVICES-PLAN.md) |
| Sunday | Tech tip |
| Monday | Install done / behind the scenes |
| Tuesday | Quick poll |
| Wednesday | Before / after |
| Thursday | Ask us anything |
| Friday | Client review |
| Saturday | Free demo offer |

- **Replies:** reply to every poll vote and question with a DM. Each one is a warm lead.
- **Highlights:** add your best Stories to the matching Highlight so they stay on your profile.
