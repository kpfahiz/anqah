# Anqah Tech: Instagram posting timeline (Oct 2026)

**How English and Arabic are combined**
- **Single-image posts:** upload as a **2-slide carousel**, with the English image first and the Arabic image second. One post reaches both audiences without putting duplicates on the grid. If a viewer skips it, Instagram often shows the post again starting on slide 2, which is an extra chance at reach.
- **"5 signs" carousel:** already 7 slides, so the English and Arabic versions are posted **separately**, on different days.
- **Caption:** English caption, then a `—` line, then the Arabic caption. Use at most 5 hashtags in total.

**Time:** about **8:00 pm UAE time** (7:00 pm KSA). On launch day, leave about 1 hour between posts.
**Crop:** choose **4:5** in the uploader.
