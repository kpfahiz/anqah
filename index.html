Frequently asked questions

Do you provide POS software in the UAE, Saudi Arabia and India?

Yes. SELLO is our complete POS and shop management system for retail stores, restaurants and cafés, and SELLO Lite is our offline mobile POS app for small shops. Both support configurable taxes, including VAT and GST.

Does the POS work without internet?

Yes. SELLO runs on your own shop network, so billing keeps working during internet outages, and SELLO Lite stores every sale on the device and works fully offline.

Is there a POS app in Arabic?

SELLO Lite is available in English, Arabic (with right-to-left layout), Spanish, French and Hindi.

What is your automatic school bell system?

A WiFi bell controller that rings on a daily timetable, skips weekends and holidays, and is managed from a mobile app — including a manual ring for assemblies and drills.

How does the water tank monitoring work?

A sensor on the tank shows the live water level on your phone, and sends a Telegram message when the tank drops below 15% (empty) or rises above 95% (overflow).

Which IT services do you offer?

Web design and development, mobile apps, digital marketing, POS and business systems, IT infrastructure, CCTV, access control, WiFi and networking, home and gate automation, GPS tracking, electrical work and annual maintenance contracts.
